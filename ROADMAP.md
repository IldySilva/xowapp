# Future Plans

## Record
- **Live Device Capture**: Connect your iPhone or iPad via USB to record real hardware—Face ID, Dynamic Island, and all.
- **Screen & Window Recording**: Record your full screen or any macOS window with full alpha (transparent background) support.
- **Dual capture**: Screen and live iPhone in one take.
- **iPhone Mirroring Beta**: Record the iPhone Mirroring app with automatic crop and device bezel.
- **Webcam & microphone**: Record yourself alongside the demo.
- **Styled overlays, multiple layouts**: Pre-built layouts for complex recordings.
- **Menu bar capture**: Clean and isolated menu bar recordings.
- **Record devices without opening the app**: Background capture agents.
- **Landscape & rotation**: Auto-detected, animatable in the timeline.

## Edit
- **Timeline Editor**: Trim, split, and stack up to 4 video tracks—or let Auto Zoom do the camera work.
- **Auto captions**: Whisper-powered transcription with a built-in transcript editor. Generated entirely locally on your Mac—no cloud, no waiting. Edit the transcript word by word.
- **Audio tracks**: Detach, trim, mix, and hide independently.
- **Censor regions**: Blur or cover sensitive areas.
- **Clip speed**: From slow motion up to 800%.
- **Playback Speed Control**: Control preview speed while editing.
- **Presets**: Start fast with built-in composition and text presets—or save your own and reuse them across every project.

## Polish
- **Device Frames**: Realistic bezels for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch—plus shadows and backgrounds.
- **Frameless Mode & Window Recording**: Support a "Frameless" style (rounded corners and smooth drop shadows without a physical bezel) for arbitrary window recordings or clean full-screen recordings. Full-screen captures can toggle between Frameless or physical Device Frames.
- **Text Overlays**: Up to 5 tracks of animated text for titles and callouts, with resizable boxes that reflow as you type.
- **Taps & Swipes**: No cursor data? Add tap and swipe indicators exactly where the action happens automatically or manually.
- **Auto Zoom**: Automatic zoom and pan on the action.
- **Custom animation curves**: Fine-tune zoom, text, clip, and rotation easing.
- **Motion blur**: Cinematic motion on every animation.
- **Zoom background blur**: Adds depth while zoomed in.
- **Background gallery**: Wallpapers, gradients, and patterns.
- **Cursor styling**: Smoothing, shadows, tints, motion blur, auto-hide.
- **Animated backgrounds**: Dynamic and moving backgrounds.
- **Adaptive color background**: Automatically matches the content color.

## Export
- **4K & Transparent Export**: Export 4K in HEVC, H.264, ProRes, or GIF—with alpha channel support.
- **Export range**: Render just a slice of the timeline.
- **Custom sizes**: 1080p, 1440p, 4K, or your own.
- **Lossless preset**: For when file size doesn't matter.
- **PNG stills**: Frames to file or clipboard.
- **Link Sharing**: Instantly share a link to the video.
- **Auto upload**: Automatically upload exports to cloud/hosting.
